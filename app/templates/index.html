Appointment Procedure

  1. Patient Enrollment: Register once with basic demographic details and emergency contact.
  2. Physician & Slot Selection: Choose clinical department and pick an available date and time slot.
  3. Check-in & Consultation: Arrive at the OPD reception on time for doctor consultation.
  4. Digital Prescription: Doctor enters clinical findings and prescription directly to your profile.
Need assistance? Contact the reception desk at (555) 019-2834 or visit Hall A, Ground Floor.
